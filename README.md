# PayPause

**Think Before Money Moves.**

PayPause is an intelligent payment safety and financial assistance platform designed for Indian consumers.

## Core Modules

- PayGuard — UPI fraud detection and payment safety.
- BillSense — Electricity bill intelligence and analysis.
- Payment Passport — Structured payment risk assessment.
- Payment Management — Transaction history, refunds and recurring payments.
- Recovery Desk — Incident management and complaint assistance.
- Flagsmith Experimentation — Real A/B testing of payment intelligence experiences.

## Technology Stack

- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- Database: Supabase / PostgreSQL
- AI: Pluggable AI provider
- Experimentation: Flagsmith
- Frontend: To be integrated
